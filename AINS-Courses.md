# AINS graduate courses — descriptions

Course-level descriptions for the **AINS** college graduate AI catalog, used alongside the sibling `programs` site. Castalia licenses **courses**; partner institutions define program titles, degrees, and how credits stack.

## Reference stack (institution-defined)

One coherent layout partners often map to a ~36-credit graduate sequence:

- `36` total credit hours (reference)
- `27` core credit hours (reference)
- `9` specialization credit hours (reference)
- `100%` online delivery in the current course designs

A reference path completes core courses then one specialization cluster—your catalog committee sets the official rules.

All `AINS` course codes below sit under the **AINS** college (**College of Artificial & Inquiring Systems**), aligned with Castalia LMS categories.

## Core Courses

### AINS6001 - Foundations of Artificial Intelligence

Introduces the fundamental concepts, history, and applications of AI, including search algorithms, knowledge representation, reasoning, and problem-solving. Emphasizes the interdisciplinary nature of AI and its role in modern industries.

### AINS6002 - Machine Learning & Predictive Modeling

Covers supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning methods for data-driven decision-making. Students learn to build and evaluate predictive models using real-world datasets and statistical techniques.

### AINS6003 - Deep Learning & Neural Networks

Explores neural network architectures such as convolutional, recurrent, and transformer models. Focuses on training techniques, optimization methods, and applications in vision, speech, and generative AI.

### AINS6004 - Natural Language Processing

Provides a comprehensive overview of text processing, sentiment analysis, language modeling, and conversational AI. Students gain experience building NLP systems using modern libraries and transformer-based models.

### AINS6005 - AI Ethics, Law & Policy

Examines the ethical, legal, and societal implications of AI. Topics include bias mitigation, privacy, transparency, accountability, and emerging regulations in AI governance.

### AINS6006 - Big Data Management for AI Applications

Covers the design and use of large-scale data systems for AI, including data pipelines, distributed processing, cloud integration, and database optimization to support AI workflows.

### AINS6007 - Applied AI Programming with Python

Teaches practical AI programming skills using Python and its AI/ML libraries. Students develop end-to-end AI applications, from data preprocessing to model deployment.

### AINS6008 - AI Project Management & Deployment

Focuses on managing AI projects from inception to production. Includes agile methodologies, stakeholder communication, resource allocation, and deploying AI solutions at scale.

### AINS6009 - Capstone Project

Students complete an industry-sponsored or student-designed AI project within their chosen specialization. The capstone integrates technical, ethical, and project management skills to deliver a fully implemented AI solution addressing a real-world challenge.

## Certificate Course

### AINS6010 - Local AI & Deployment to Hardware

Focuses on deploying AI models to actual hardware: edge devices, on-premises servers, and embedded systems. Covers running models locally, hardware-aware optimization, resource constraints, and deployment pipelines for non-cloud environments. Stackable certificate course.

## Specialization: Healthcare AI

### AINS6100 - AI in Medical Imaging

Explores AI applications in radiology and pathology, including image classification, segmentation, and anomaly detection.

### AINS6101 - Predictive Analytics in Population Health

Covers statistical modeling and AI tools for disease prediction, patient stratification, and healthcare trend analysis.

### AINS6102 - AI for Clinical Decision Support

Focuses on AI systems that assist clinicians with diagnostics, treatment planning, and personalized medicine.

## Specialization: Business AI

### AINS6200 - AI for Marketing & Customer Insights

Teaches the use of AI for customer segmentation, recommendation engines, and campaign optimization.

### AINS6201 - Automation & Process Optimization

Examines robotic process automation, workflow optimization, and AI-driven business efficiency tools.

### AINS6202 - AI Strategy for Executives

Provides frameworks for integrating AI into business strategy, investment planning, and competitive positioning.

## Specialization: Cybersecurity AI

### AINS6300 - AI in Threat Detection

Explores AI methods for identifying cyber threats, anomalies, and intrusion attempts.

### AINS6301 - Automated Response Systems

Covers AI-driven automation of incident response and remediation strategies.

### AINS6302 - AI for Risk Assessment

Teaches AI-based risk modeling and predictive analysis for cybersecurity decision-making.

## Specialization: Robotics AI

### AINS6400 - Robot Perception & Spatial AI

Covers sensing, representation, and learning for robots operating in physical environments, including vision, depth, mapping, and spatial reasoning for manipulation and navigation.

### AINS6401 - Motion Planning, Control & Learning for Autonomous Systems

Integrates classical planning and control with learning-based policies for legged, wheeled, and articulated platforms, emphasizing safety, sim-to-real transfer, and deployment constraints.

### AINS6402 - Multi-Robot Systems & Human-Robot Interaction

Explores coordination, fleet-level autonomy, shared world models, and human-in-the-loop design for collaborative robotics in industrial and service settings.
