# AIMA5001: Classroom

**Source:** `web/myst-sources/ain2001/classroom.md` → `/demos/myst/ain2001/classroom/`.

## Product

**GitHub Classroom** distributes private assignment repos; **devcontainers** (Codespaces or local) give every student the same Python environment, tests, and autograding hooks.

```{note}
Assignments and rubrics remain authorable in MyST in the course book; Classroom is the delivery and submission rail.
```
